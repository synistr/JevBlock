The harbour gets a new electric ferry

The city council approved the purchase of a battery-electric ferry on Tuesday. It will replace the diesel boat that has run between the old town and the island since 1987.

Crossings will take about twelve minutes. The operator expects the ferry to enter service next spring after sea trials.
